# Research overview

The question is whether explicit forecast discrepancies become predictive after task consequences are known. State accuracy, observational response dependence, action consistency, task relevance, outcome prediction and causal faithfulness are separate properties.

The experiment is a bounded empirical investigation around one frozen public world-model agent. Its contribution is an implemented controlled comparison, preservation of actual outputs and transparent measurement limits. It does not claim a new training method or that reasoning collapse was newly discovered.

RAGEN-2 already studies reasoning collapse and cross-input diagnostics. VAGEN and SPA study world-model state/transition reasoning or supervision. This project asks a narrower incremental-prediction question. The relation to Professor Li's work is intellectual relevance, not supervision or collaboration.

Recorded signals and derived diagnostics are separated in the editable graphical abstract. The roadmap is explicitly proposed future work. No follow-up experiment is reported as completed.
